I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Toronto, advised by [Dr. Babak Taati](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~taati/). I work on computer vision and vision-language models. My PhD is funded by an NSERC Canada Graduate Scholarship (CGS-D).

I am a Faculty Affiliate Researcher at the [Vector Institute](https://vectorinstitute.ai/) and a Research Appointee at the [KITE Research Institute](https://kite-uhn.com/) at the University Health Network, where I work with the KITE Aging team on the Ambient project.

#### Research

My recent work asks:

- **Can diffusion transformers generate at resolutions they were never trained on, without retraining?** [SEGA](https://rajabi2001.github.io/sega/) scales attention per RoPE frequency band using the spectral energy of the latent at each denoising step. Accepted to NeurIPS 2026. [Project page](https://rajabi2001.github.io/sega/)

Before my PhD, I worked on machine learning for quantum field theory and combinatorics: predicting Feynman periods in φ⁴-theory with [Dr. Paul-Hermann Balduf](https://paulbalduf.com/) ([JHEP 2024](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/JHEP11(2024)038>)), the statistics of subdivergence-free Feynman integrals ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06898)), and witnesses in Covtree with [Dr. Rowan Yeats](https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~kayeats/) ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00622)). The full list is on the [publications](/publications/) page.

#### Beyond research

- I run our group's [journal club](/journal-club/), a series of paper discussions and guest talks that has been going since 2019. I set the schedule, invite speakers, and maintain the page. We meet on Tuesdays at the Vector Institute.
- I co-organized the [MoCha Workshop on Human Motion Challenges in Real-World and Clinical Settings](https://mocha.care-pd.ca/) at ECCV 2026.
- I have been a teaching assistant for a dozen courses at Toronto and Waterloo, including Lead TA for CSC110 and CSC111, and received the C&O Department Outstanding TA Award at Waterloo. See [teaching](/teaching/).

#### Background

I completed an MMath in Combinatorics & Optimization at the University of Waterloo, supervised by Dr. Rowan Yeats. My [thesis](https://uwspace.uwaterloo.ca/items/26a83106-75b2-4990-aeb2-ea94bd680a11) is on combinatorial aspects of Feynman integrals and causal set theory. Before that I did a BMath at Waterloo, with a research term on deep Q-learning for antichains in Coxeter groups with Prof. Oliver Pechenik.

I grew up in Waterloo, Ontario. Outside of research I play piano, hike with my dog, and play Tetris.
